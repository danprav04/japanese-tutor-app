## Curriculum Quality Analysis

Overall, the curriculum is a **solid foundation** that accurately captures the core grammar from the lesson file, but has a number of notable issues worth addressing.

---

### ✅ Strengths

**Good coverage of core grammar concepts.** The state-of-being forms (だ, だった, じゃない, じゃなかった), particles (は, も, が, を, に, で, と, の, や), verb classifications, past tense conjugations, and adverb forms are all present and align well with what the lesson teaches.

**Examples are mostly accurate.** Most grammar entries use examples directly from or consistent with the source material (e.g., 誰が学生？, ボブは魚が好きだ, etc.).

**Appropriate JLPT leveling.** The JLPT 5 → 4 → 3 progression is reasonable given the content's increasing complexity.

---

### ⚠️ Issues Found

**1. Missing grammar items from the lesson**
Several concepts taught explicitly in the lesson are absent from the curriculum:
- The **「ね」 and 「よ」 sentence-ending particles** — the lesson has a full section on these, but they're nowhere in the curriculum.
- **「よね」 combination particle** — also missing.
- **The implied state-of-being** (using no copula at all, e.g., 元気？→ 元気。) is a key concept explicitly called out in the lesson, but absent.
- **「の」 nominalization** is listed but the lesson gives more context on how it works as an explanatory particle vs. possession — only possession is captured.

**2. Incorrect/misleading example for 「も」**
The curriculum entry for も gives the example *ジョンは明日。* with translation *John is tomorrow.* — but this sentence uses は, not も. This is a copy-paste error from the は section and is factually wrong for a も entry.

**3. Incorrect reading for 「かわいい」 vocab entry**
The reading for *かわいい* (cute) is listed as おも・し・ろい, which is the reading for *面白い* (interesting). This is a clear data error.

**4. Kanji entries with questionable accuracy**
- **あ, い, る** are listed as kanji but they are hiragana. These likely represent ある and いる and should be labeled differently.
- **部 (ぶ)** has the kunyomi listed as へや (room), but 部 means "section/department" — へや is 部屋, a compound word. This conflates the kanji with its common compound.
- **話 (はな)** has the onyomi listed as ワダ — it should be ワ (話す is the kunyomi form). ワダ is incorrect.
- **閉** has onyomi listed as ヒエ, which is wrong. The correct onyomi is ヘイ.
- **雑** has onyomi listed as ツマ, which is wrong. The correct onyomi is ザツ/ゾウ.
- **落** has onyomi listed as ロク — incorrect, it should be ラク.

**5. Misleading translation for 「行く」 vocab**
The entry for 行く (to go) has the example 家へ帰らない with the translation *Not go home toward house* — but 帰る means "to return home," not 行く (to go). The example word doesn't match the vocab being taught.

**6. 「たくさん」 reading error**
The reading for たくさん is listed as たんまり, which is a completely different word (meaning "to one's heart's content"). This is incorrect.

**7. JLPT level inconsistencies**
Some items feel misleveled. For example, んだ and relative clauses (N3-level) are appropriate, but some N4 kanji like 立入 are compound words rather than individual kanji and may confuse learners.
